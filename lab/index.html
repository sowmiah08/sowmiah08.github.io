[02] About

From pixels
to grasps.

I'm a robotics engineer with an MSc in Robotics Engineering from the University of Bristol and UWE Bristol, and a background in computer science and machine learning.

Most of my work sits where perception meets manipulation: segmenting objects in RGB-D data, estimating their 6-DoF pose, and handing that to a motion planner that can actually reach it. I build on ROS 2 and MoveIt 2, and test on real SO-101 arms as well as in Gazebo.

I care about the parts that decide whether a demo becomes a system: frame transforms, controller tolerances, recovery when a grasp misses, and evaluation against ground truth instead of a single good run.

01

Verify, don't assume

Randomised trials scored against simulator ground truth, not one lucky run.

02

Design for failure

Re-scan, retry the grasp, spiral-search, or report "not found" honestly.

03

Keep it configurable

New parts are added through a model and a YAML file, with no new code.
