Simulation · Dual-arm assembly

visionNassembly

Completed · working demo in simulation

Two SO-101 arms use an overhead RGB-D camera to find parts, estimate their 6-DoF pose to about 1 mm, and assemble a plug into a socket and a peg into a plate. It recovers from failures on its own. 11 of 12 randomised trials succeeded.

Real hardware · Dual-arm manipulation

Dual-arm pick & place on SO-101

Right arm complete · left arm calibration next

One RealSense camera, two real SO-101 arms. HSV + DBSCAN + a cube-shape filter find cubes in the point cloud, a TF router picks the arm, and an async MoveIt 2 state machine grasps, lifts and drops each cube in a bin.

Real hardware · HRI

Hand-guided teleoperation

Completed · working on hardware

Point a finger and the SO-ARM100 follows. MediaPipe finds the fingertip, depth back-projects it into 3D, an AprilTag gives the camera-to-robot transform, and LeRobot IK drives the motors, with safety limits.

Agri-robotics · Deep learning

Apple detection for yield estimation

Completed · exported to ONNX

A YOLOv8 experiment on combined MinneApple + Kaggle + custom data, then a deployed YOLO11s run reaching 0.853 mAP@50 (P 0.82, R 0.75) on 6,172 validation apples, exported to ONNX for CPU inference.

RRR end-effector control
