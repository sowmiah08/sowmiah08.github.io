Dual-arm pick & place on SO-101

One RealSense camera, two real SO-101 arms. A classical point-cloud pipeline finds coloured cubes, a TF router decides which arm should pick each one, and each arm runs an asynchronous MoveIt 2 state machine that grasps, lifts and drops the cube in a bin.

Async everywhere

Everything after the point cloud is asynchronous ROS 2 callback chains, with no blocking time.sleep(). A sleep in a callback stalls unrelated topics such as /joint_states, which MoveIt needs.

One shared planning scene

The SRDF defines two base→jaw planning groups, with generated disable-collision pairs so neighbouring links aren't treated as permanently colliding.

Perception · three design decisions

Point cloud → "a cube at (x, y, z)"

01 · HSV, NOT RGB

Hue separates colour from brightness, so thresholds survive lighting changes. Red: h < 10 or > 170. Blue: 100 < h < 140.

02 · MASK, THEN DBSCAN

Cluster only the colour-masked points: eps = 2 cm, min 30 points. No need to know how many cubes are present, and sparse noise is dropped.

03 · SHAPE FILTER

_is_cube_like: every axis 3 ± 1.5 cm. It rejects elongated (> 6 cm, wires) and flat (< 1.5 cm, glare) clusters.

Known limitation

Two touching same-colour cubes merge into one oversized cluster, fail the size filter and are silently dropped: a false negative, not a crash. This is classical CV that works because the task is constrained (known size, colours, static camera).

The dual-arm decision

Which arm picks this cube?

p = transform(cube, "right_base_link")
if p.y > 0.15:          # metres
    publish(transform(cube, "left_base_link"),
            "/cube_left_base")
else:
    publish(p, "/cube_right_base")

Why it's good

Cheap and deterministic. One detector feeds both arms, and no per-arm perception is needed.

What it isn't

A proxy for reachability, not a guarantee. A cube near y ≈ 0.15 can be routed to an arm that can't reach it, and there's no fallback to the other arm.

BUFFER + LOCK

Averaging 10 centroids stops the arm twitching. A busy/locked guard stops a new detection re-entering mid-sequence.

HYBRID PLANNING

Grasp and lift use collision-aware MoveGroup goals (dynamic target). The bin move is a raw joint trajectory (fixed, pre-validated target).

STATE-AWARE FAILURE

A failed grasp unlocks and restarts. A failed lift opens the gripper and goes home, since it may be holding a cube.

MoveIt goals · real hardware

Constraints, drift, and the servo bridge

Goals as constraints

  • 5 cm box around the jaw-link target, because asking for more precision than the camera gives only makes planning fail
  • Top-down [0, −0.707, 0, 0.707]: 0.3 rad tolerance for grasp, 0.5 rad for lift and bin

The CONTROL_FAILED bug

After the gripper closed, the arm joints drifted slightly and MoveIt rejected the lift. Fix: is_diff = True plus allowed_start_tolerance: 0.0, which trusts the live state. That's a deliberate, scoped trade-off.

Servo bridge

FollowJointTrajectory becomes Feetech serial writes: ticks 0–4095 ↔ [−π, π), per-joint calibration offsets (measured on the right arm), and joint states published at 20 Hz.
