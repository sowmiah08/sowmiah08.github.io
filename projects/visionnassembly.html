Why cluster on footprint only?

From above, the plug body and its knob have no visible side joining them, so 3D clustering split the plug in two. The x, y footprint keeps it together.

The occluder leak

SAM 2 works on colour, so a mask could include an occluder in front of a part, making it look 25 cm tall. Fix: keep only mask points inside the height band.

Perception · pose

6-DoF pose by model fitting (ICP)

  • Point-to-plane ICP in two stages: 12 mm then 4 mm matching distance
  • Several starting yaws (every 15° within the symmetry period) to avoid local minima
  • Only camera-facing model points, which fixed a 12 mm top/bottom-face mix-up that still scored a near-perfect fit
  • Table prior: a part on the table rests on it, leaving only x, y and yaw to find
  • Fitness ≥ 0.8 means "found", otherwise "missing" with a reason

From simulation to a real 3D camera

What changes with a real point cloud

Hand-eye calibration

Overhead camera = eye-to-hand; wrist cameras = eye-in-hand. Several robot poses viewing a target → solve AX = XB. Free in simulation, the first job on hardware.

Better clouds → better fits

Structured light is very precise at close range; ToF has multipath; stereo needs texture. Fewer missing points matter most under occlusion.

Reflective & dark parts

Filter outliers, let colour (SAM 2) define the object where depth is missing, fit fewer reliable points with priors, take more views.

Limits → next

Simulation only, 12 trials, small reach, <20% visibility missed. Next: wrist-camera active perception, bigger evaluations, a learned-pose baseline.
